Avatar QA harness

Every control here bypasses a wait. Nothing needs 30 seconds.

Poses

Poses cycle on their own at a moderate pace with a 420ms eased crossfade. Clicking a pose interrupts the cycle for ~2.2s then hands back. Pupils and blink run on idle-still only: the layers are cut from that frame, and every other pose is a separate render with its irises in a different place (measured mean error 18–58 vs 1.6 for idle-still), so overlaying them draws a visible second set of irises.

Sequences & state

Toggles

Reduced motion stops breathing, parallax, tilt, pupils, blink and the video — not just the video. Force-video skips the 30s wait but still honours the VP9-alpha probe, so Safari will correctly show the static poster instead.

After destroy the fps readout must stop and the stage must go empty — that is the check for leaked rAF, timers and listeners.
